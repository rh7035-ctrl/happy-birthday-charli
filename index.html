🌙 A Little Secret

If you've found this...
Then you've discovered the only hidden page in this little universe.

I didn't want this to be part of your birthday because today is about celebrating you.

But I wanted to leave one tiny wish somewhere among the stars.

Next year you'll turn twenty-one.
Maybe life will take us in completely different directions.
Maybe it won't.
None of us can really know.

But if, somehow, life lets our paths cross again...
I'd still love to celebrate your twenty-first birthday with you.

Maybe we'd spend the day exploring somewhere new.
Maybe we'd laugh over terrible food.
Maybe we'd go shopping.
Maybe we'd simply watch the sunset and talk for hours.

And if life has different plans...
Then that's okay too.

I hope your 20th birthday is filled with people who make you smile, wherever you are.

Some wishes don't need to become plans.
They're simply beautiful because they exist.

✨

Made with love.
Happy Birthday. ✨
